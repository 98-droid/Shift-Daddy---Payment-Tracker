Backup & data

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on this phone, in this app or browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. That also means clearing Safari website data, or deleting the home-screen app, erases it, so copy a backup now and then.

Restore a backup

Paste a backup you copied earlier, then restore. This replaces what is currently on this device.

Put it on your home screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen.”
  3. Open it from the icon and enter your jobs there. The home-screen app keeps its own storage, separate from the Safari tab, so add it first, then start logging.
  4. On Android, open the link in Chrome instead and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.”

After the first open, the whole app is saved on the phone and launches with no connection at all.
